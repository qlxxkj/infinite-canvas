What is a free AI video generator?

A free AI video generator turns your input — a photo, a short text prompt, or both — into a moving video clip. Infinite Canvas does this on an infinite canvas: every generated clip is a node you can connect, remix, and reason over, instead of one-off exports you can't trace back.

Text to video, for free

Describe a scene in plain language and generate a clip. Compare several takes side by side on the canvas, then keep the one that matches your idea.

Why self-hosted matters

Self-hosting means no per-clip metering, no third-party limits, and your media never leaves your infrastructure. Free in the way that lasts — not a capped trial.
