How image to video works

Drop an image onto the canvas, add a motion prompt (camera move, subject motion, lighting), and generate. The result is a video node you can edit, restyle, or chain into the next shot.

Use cases

Bring product photos to life for ads, animate avatars and portraits, turn illustrations and posters into looping motion, or storyboard a scene one clip at a time.

No watermark, no limits

Because it is self-hosted, your clips are yours — no forced watermark, no export cap, and no per-second pricing. Generate as many takes as you need and keep the best.
